How this collab works

One channel, one home: content is published on the Radio RRR YouTube channel. No mirrored uploads of the same video elsewhere (algorithm hates that).

Revenue share: split is based on watch time (the fair way). If/when the channel is monetized and earning revenue, contributors are paid monthly based on their % of total watch time.

What I’m looking for

  • Original mixes / sets (with clean rights — more on that below)
  • Visualizer videos for original tracks
  • Genre-focused shows (electro / synthwave / chill / retro)
  • Live-stream segments (if you’ve got the setup)
  • Gear / synth / production mini content (fast + punchy, not boring)

The simple rules (so nobody loses their mind)

  • Transparency: monthly stats shared (watch time + revenue screenshot).
  • Payments: monthly (PayID/PayPal) once there’s real revenue.
  • Quality bar: I curate what goes up. This isn’t a free-for-all.
  • Exit clause: either side can end future uploads with notice. No drama.

Non-negotiable: music rights

If your content uses copyrighted tracks without permission, it will likely get claimed or demonetized. That’s not “maybe” — that’s YouTube life.

Best options:

  • Original music (yours or licensed from the artist)
  • Royalty-free / licensed libraries
  • Explicit written permission from rights holders

What to include in your pitch

1) Your creator name + links (TikTok/YouTube/IG)
2) What you want to make (2–3 formats)
3) Music sources + rights (original / licensed / permission?)
4) Upload frequency you can actually sustain
5) Any samples (even rough drafts)

FAQ

“Can I still post the same video on my own channel?”
Ideally no — duplicate uploads split attention and usually underperform. If you want to post a teaser, do a shorter cutdown that points to the Radio RRR full version.

“When does revenue sharing start?”
When the channel is actually earning. Until then: we build the library, the audience, and the vibe.

“What if my video gets claimed?”
Then it may earn $0. If it’s constantly happening, we stop publishing that format. Simple.
