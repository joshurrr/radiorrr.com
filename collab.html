How this works

One channel, one home: content is published on the Radio RRR YouTube channel. No “same upload on both channels” — the algorithm hates that. Teasers are fine.

Revenue share: split based on watch time once the channel is monetized and earning. Paid monthly. Stats shared monthly. Simple.

What I’m looking for

  • Visualizer videos for original tracks
  • Live / DJ-style sets (with clean rights — see below)
  • Genre-focused shows (electro / synthwave / chill / retro)
  • Edits + short-form that fit the Radio RRR vibe
  • Synth / gear / production mini content (fast + useful)

The simple rules (so it doesn’t turn into drama)

  • Transparency: monthly screenshot of watch time + revenue numbers.
  • Payments: monthly payouts once there’s real money coming in.
  • Quality control: I curate uploads. This isn’t a free-for-all.
  • Exit clause: either side can stop future uploads with notice.

FAQ

Can I also post the same video on my own channel?
Ideally no. Do a teaser/cutdown on your channel and point people to the Radio RRR version.

When does revenue sharing start?
When the channel is earning. Until then: we build the catalog and audience.

What if a video gets claimed?
Then it may earn $0. If it’s constantly happening, we stop using that content style.
