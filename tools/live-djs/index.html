Live Stream BPM Detector

Detect the BPM of a live online audio or HLS stream. Paste a direct HTTP or HTTPS stream URL and Radio RRR will analyse a short audio sample to estimate its tempo.

This tool is designed for DJs, online radio stations and live music streams where you want a quick BPM estimate without downloading the audio first.

DJ Speech Detector

Detect talking in live DJ streams using voice-versus-music analysis. Radio RRR measures repeated microphone speech and music ducking so brief announcements are treated differently from persistent talking.

The detector scans Radio RRR's latest live DJ audio samples and reports talk activity for the DJs currently being monitored.

HLS Stream Health Test

Test the stability of Radio RRR's current live HLS playback for 60 seconds. The browser monitor records buffering events, total buffering time, dropped frames, HLS errors and recovery after a DJ switch.

The test is passive: it observes the existing Radio RRR live player instead of opening a second stream, so the measurements reflect the playback session you are actually using.

What the stream test measures

Use the results to spot repeated buffering, long stalls, excessive dropped frames or slow recovery when the live relay changes from one DJ to another.

Stuck Frame Effect

Upload a video and Radio RRR will analyse its soundtrack, detect the beat and add short stuck-frame freezes in time with the music.

The video is processed on the Radio RRR server and returned as a new MP4. Audio timing is preserved while the visual freeze effect is applied to beat positions.
